## 2. What spectacled is

spectacled is a family of apps for journals, notes and tasks based on the open standards CalDAV and iCalendar. The apps store your data on your device and synchronize it with a **CalDAV server of your choice**. We do not provide CalDAV storage and do not operate user accounts.

For the web app, we offer an optional hosted proxy (`spectacled-proxy.fly.dev`) for users who cannot run their own. It relays traffic between your browser and your CalDAV server. As described in the [Privacy Policy](https://spectacled.techbee.at/privacy-policy/), it technically has access to your credentials and entries while they pass through.

- The hosted proxy is provided free of charge, independently of whether you purchased an app, and on a best-effort basis. We do not guarantee its availability, and we may limit, change or discontinue it at any time.
- You may only use it to access CalDAV servers you are authorized to access.
- We may block requests or access in case of abuse, excessive use or security risks.
- We recommend running your own proxy; instructions are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/TechbeeAT/spectacled/tree/main/server).
